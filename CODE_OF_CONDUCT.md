# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, welcome people with different experience levels, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest where possible.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or, when confidentiality is important, a private GitHub security advisory. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against reporters is not permitted.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
